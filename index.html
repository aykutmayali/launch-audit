Stackify Retrace · End of Life · 31 March 2027

Retrace shuts down: I move .NET apps to OpenTelemetry

BMC Helix is retiring Stackify Retrace on 31 March 2027. Because it is SaaS-only, you will not be able to log in, send data, or read stored data after that date. There is no automated migration.

I inventory Retrace agents, profilers, log appenders and custom instrumentation in your .NET solution (including IIS / .NET Framework), replace them with OpenTelemetry, and land the data on Azure Monitor, Grafana or another OTLP backend you choose — without locking you to a single APM vendor.
